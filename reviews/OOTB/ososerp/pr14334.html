F2 src/Modules/Project/Project.Domain/Models/ProjectStakeholder.cs:29

Mandatory enums default silently to Low. The story says Influence and Interest are mandatory. As non-nullable enums with Low = 0, an omitted JSON field or a NOT NULL default of 0 stores 'Low'. The create/update validators (still to come) cannot detect omission, and the value out of the enum range is not rejected at the DB level. Consider a nullable type with a NotNull validator, or making High/Low start at 1. Also add IsInEnum validators for all four enum fields when the commands are added. Title has no length or required constraint, and all string columns are unbounded text/nvarchar(max).
