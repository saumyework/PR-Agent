This PR adds a Fleet traffic violation feature: a service, extensions, endpoints, and CRUD, list, unique-code and assigned-drivers use cases. Lookups are company-scoped and delete is a soft delete.

Merge is blocked by two problems. VehicleId and OperatorId are not validated against the caller's company, which allows cross-tenant references and leaks. The by-id lookup also includes soft-deleted rows, so removed violations can be read, updated and deleted again.

The team should also decide how to generate codes (random versus DocumentCodeSetup) and whether to add a unique index. The PR description is empty, and no tests or migration are visible in the diff.

Method: 4 independent review calls over 1 shard(s), evidence machine-checked (0 unverifiable candidates discarded), then one merge call.
